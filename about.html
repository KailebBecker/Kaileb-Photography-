The person behind the camera

A little about how I got here, and what I try to do with every session.

My story

I picked up a camera to document the people and moments closest to me, and it turned into a business built on the same idea: showing up fully for the people in front of my lens. Every wedding, proposal, graduation, car, and portrait shoot I do starts with the same goal — capture it the way it actually felt, not just the way it looked.

I'm based in Arizona and shoot mostly outdoors, working with natural light and the desert's golden hour whenever I can. I keep sessions relaxed and low-pressure — genuine expressions and real moments almost always beat a stiff, posed shot.

Whether it's a full wedding day, a proposal I'm capturing candidly from a distance, a graduation session, a car shoot built around the details, or a portrait sitting, my approach stays the same: pay attention, stay out of the way when I need to, and hand you back a set of photos that actually feel like you.
